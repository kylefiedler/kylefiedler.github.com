{%- comment -%} Turns the Dryer page into the inside of a dryer. A click on the page's .scatter (not on a link) flings every drawing out to the drum's wall, carries them all the same way around it, spinning as they go, and drops each one in a new spot clear of the heading, the list and the other drawings. The drum is the window below the nav's top rule on wide screens (keep `wide` in step with $wide) and the on-screen part of .scatter below that rule on narrow ones, fixed when the click lands. Each drawing's position blends from where it was, to the wall, to its new spot, which is what makes it peel off the wall rather than turn a corner. Drawings stay in their grid cells and move by `translate`, measured from the cell (`offsetLeft`, so .scatter--dryer is their offset parent). After a tumble their spots are kept as a share of .scatter, so a resize stretches them and any pushed onto the text find a new spot. Reduced motion turns it off. {%- endcomment -%}
