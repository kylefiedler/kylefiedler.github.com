{%- comment -%} Lets a mouse drag the scraps on the page's .scraps board. A drag writes the same --x/--y the stylesheet places scraps with, so a moved scrap scales with the pile and is ignored once the scraps stack. Drags never start on a field, button or link, so a writable scrap still takes clicks. A finished drag fires `scrap:moved` on the scrap. {%- endcomment -%}
