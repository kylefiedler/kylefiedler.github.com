{%- comment -%} The photo wall on Split pages. `page.wall` names a set in _data/walls/ (written by script/wall-photos) and falls back to the gray placeholder set. Entries without a `src` render as gray tiles. Photos are dealt round-robin into three columns, cycling through the set until every photo is used and each column is at least `column_min` of its own widths tall. When the set is a multiple of 3, each pass through it shifts by one photo; otherwise every column would cycle the same third. The narrowest column is about 70px wide at the 768px breakpoint, so 16 widths outrun a 1024px-tall window plus the columns' stagger. Each column's track holds two copies of its tiles, and the drift moves it by exactly one copy, so the loop has no seam. Phones get one still strip instead, cycled until it is at least `strip_min` of its own heights wide, comfortably past the widest phone layout. Screen readers get each photo's alt once: on its first appearance in the columns, or in the strip on phones. Repeats and the second copy are silent, and so are photos whose alt is empty. `wall: static` renders three empty, still columns that scroll with the page, for case studies to fill with their own media. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if page.wall == "static" -%}
