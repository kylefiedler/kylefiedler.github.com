Things I’ve found in the dryer

My kids leave a lot of weird things in their pockets and they all come out in the dryer. They’re little suprises for me every time I do laundry.

  • Sea shells
  • Mulch
  • Gravel
  • Geode
  • Lego pieces
  • Loose change
  • Dollar bill
  • Camera lanyard
  • Switch game cartridge
  • Action figure
  • Dead insect
