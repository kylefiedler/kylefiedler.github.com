Catabolic

An opinionated time-restricted eating tracker for iOS, designed in the spirit of Braun.

What it does

Catabolic tracks a single fasting window, opinionated around the 13–16 hour range supported by current research. No streaks, no social feed, no nagging — just a clear timer, a goal, and a quiet log of past fasts.

Support

Questions, bugs, or feedback? Email kyle@kylefiedler.com and I’ll get back to you.

Privacy

Catabolic stores your fasting data on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, there are no analytics, and there are no third-party trackers.
